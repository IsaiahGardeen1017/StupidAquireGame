Field manual

How to play

01

Place a tile

Connect isolated tiles to found a hotel, grow an existing hotel, or join hotels and trigger a merger. Hotels with 11 or more tiles are safe and cannot be taken over.

02

Resolve mergers

The largest hotel survives. Investors in the acquired hotel receive majority and minority bonuses, then their shares are sold. Ties split bonuses evenly.

03

Buy stock

After placing, buy up to three available shares across active hotels. Prices rise as hotels grow. You may also pass.

04

End & win

Once every active hotel is safe, or one hotel reaches 41 tiles, you may end the game. All remaining hotels pay bonuses and shares are sold. Highest net worth wins.

Boardroom follows the classic 9 × 12 hotel investment rules. This fan-made implementation is not affiliated with any publisher.

Pass the device

Your hand and finances are private.
